Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

In short

  • Your brewery records stay in your own browser. I never receive them.
  • There are no accounts, so I hold no passwords or profiles.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you choose Allow. You can change your mind at any time in Settings.
  • Questions or requests go to napaankur@gmail.com.

Who is responsible

Brewhouse ERP is a free web application made and run by Ankur Napa ("I" or "me"). For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 I am the data fiduciary for the limited personal data described below. You can reach me at napaankur@gmail.com.

Your brewery data

Everything you enter in Brewhouse ERP is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. That includes recipes, batches, stock, customers, vendors, invoices, tax figures and settings. It is not sent to me or to any server I control. I cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.

Clearing your browser data, using a private window or changing device removes or hides it. Backups you export are files under your control. Please export a backup regularly.

If you enter details of other people in the app, such as customer names, phone numbers or GSTINs, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to hold them. They stay on your device.

Analytics, only with your consent

When you first open the app it asks whether you allow anonymous analytics. Nothing is loaded from Google Analytics unless you choose Allow.

If you allow it, the app uses Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, to count:

  • which screens are opened, by screen name only
  • a fixed set of actions, such as loading the demo, exporting a backup, downloading a CSV, applying a brewery template or clicking the call and LinkedIn links
  • general technical details Google Analytics collects by default: browser, device type, screen size, language, referring site and approximate location at city or country level

The app never sends your brewery records to Google Analytics. No recipe names, batch codes, quantities, prices, customer details or tax figures are included in any event.

Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_1ZHW47YJDC on this site to tell visits apart. They last up to two years unless you withdraw consent or clear them. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Event data is kept for the retention period set on the Analytics property, which Google limits to 14 months at most. Google processes this data under its own terms. It may process it outside India.

The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings, under Privacy and analytics. Turning analytics off stops tracking and removes the Analytics cookies from this site. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything already counted before you withdrew it.

Other services the site loads

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • cdnjs, run by Cloudflare, serves the library that draws QR codes. Your browser fetches it from Cloudflare's servers.

If you email me

If you write to me or ask for a call, I use your name, email address and anything you tell me only to reply and to help you. I do not add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask me to tell you what personal data I hold about you, correct it, erase it or nominate someone to act for you. You can also raise a grievance. Since your brewery data never reaches me, these requests mostly concern emails you have sent me. Analytics data is pseudonymous: I cannot link it to you by name, but turning analytics off stops any further collection.

To make a request or raise a grievance, email napaankur@gmail.com. I aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with my response you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Age

Brewhouse ERP is a tool for breweries and the people who work in them. It is meant for adults of legal drinking age in their state and is not aimed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. If a change means collecting anything new, the app will ask for your consent again.
