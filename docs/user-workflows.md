# User workflows

How each person at the brewery runs their day in Brewhouse ERP. The same steps appear in the app under **Help**.

## Manager

**Every morning**

1. Open Home and read the alerts. Tap one to jump to the fix. *(Home)*
2. Check the brewery floor. Tap a tank to open its batch record. *(Home)*

**Every week**

1. Open Schedule and read the Brew planner. Plan brew picks a clean fermenter and reserves ingredients. *(Schedule)*
2. Read Reports, Business, for revenue, cost per litre and margin. *(Reports)*
3. Check Beer losses against the target. *(Beer losses)*

## Brewer

**New beer**

1. Recipes, New recipe. Watch gravity, bitterness, colour and cost update as you type. *(Recipes)*

**Brew day**

1. Make sure the fermenter is sanitised. Log CIP if it is not. *(CIP)*
2. Batches: move the planned batch to Brewing. Ingredients are issued and the brew task closes. *(Batches)*
3. Open Brew log and enter mash temperature, gravities, volume and pitch temperature. Deviations are flagged. *(Batches)*

**Cellar round**

1. Tanks: Log reading with gravity, temperature and pH. *(Tanks)*
2. Record loss for yeast dumps or dry hop loss. *(Tanks)*
3. Transfer to a brite tank. The fermenter is marked dirty and a CIP task is added. *(Tanks)*
4. Move the batch to Conditioning, then Ready. *(Batches)*

## Packaging

**Packaging run**

1. Packaging, Package beer. Pick the batch, the format and how many. *(Packaging)*
2. Enter litres drawn if more came out of the tank than went into packs. The difference is a packaging loss. *(Packaging)*
3. Kegs are picked from empty stock and tagged to the batch. *(Equipment and kegs)*

**Cleaning**

1. Log CIP on every empty tank, with chemical, temperature, minutes and the check result. *(CIP)*
2. Wash returned kegs from the keg page: Cleaned, now empty. *(Equipment and kegs)*

## Stores

**Delivery arrives**

1. Purchasing and MRP: Receive on the purchase order. The delivery is held as a pending lot. *(Purchasing and MRP)*
2. Raw materials: QC on the material, then Approve or Reject the lot. *(Raw materials)*

**Weekly**

1. Purchasing and MRP: Raise POs from plan. *(Purchasing and MRP)*
2. Raw materials: Use / waste for spillage, expired stock or samples. *(Raw materials)*

**Month end**

1. Stock take, New count. Count blind on a phone, review the variance, give each one a reason, then post. *(Stock take)*

## Taproom

**Every sale**

1. Taproom sales, New invoice. Prices fill in from the menu. *(Taproom sales)*
2. If a beer has run out the app warns before saving. *(Taproom sales)*
3. Print the tax invoice: VAT on beer, CGST and SGST on food. *(Taproom sales)*

**End of shift**

1. Record line flush and spillage as a Taproom loss on the batch. *(Beer losses)*

## Driver

**Delivering**

1. Scan the keg QR tag with the phone camera and tap Dispatch. *(Equipment and kegs)*
2. Choose the customer. A trade invoice is raised with the dispatch. *(Dispatch)*

**Collecting**

1. Dispatch, Kegs to collect, lists every keg out with each customer. *(Dispatch)*
2. Scan the empty keg at the bar and tap Picked up. *(Equipment and kegs)*

## Accounts

**Month end**

1. Excise and tax: the excise register and monthly VAT and GST, with CSV downloads. *(Excise and tax)*
2. Taproom sales: Sales CSV, trade and taproom invoices together. *(Taproom sales)*
3. Utilities and wastage: enter the month's bills so cost per litre is right. *(Utilities and wastage)*
4. Stock take: Adjustments CSV for the auditor. *(Stock take)*
5. Settings: Export backup at least weekly. *(Settings)*
